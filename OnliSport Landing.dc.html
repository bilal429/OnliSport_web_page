01 Matches & results

From open slot
to final score.

Every match follows the same path: post it, pick an opponent, play, then both sides confirm the result. No more arguing about the score in a group chat.

Scores match

When both sides submit the same score, it's accepted and goes on both teams' records.

Scores differ

The other side can accept or dispute what was submitted. A disputed result is flagged on the match.

Community vote

Players outside the match vote on a disputed result. Nobody from either team gets a say.

  • Single & Double Wicket

    Play one-on-one or in pairs. An out doesn't end the innings, so both sides bat their full overs.

  • Tape ball or hard ball

    Choose the ball when you post the match. Each ball type gets its own formats, from 4 overs to 50.

  • Your innings, your scorecard

    Each captain enters their own innings with batting and bowling figures. The other side reviews it, then accepts or disputes.

  • Batsman, bowler, all-rounder

    Players pick their cricket role, and captains build the batting order from presets like Classic, T20 Attack and Bowling Heavy.

03 Football & teams

Run your side like a club.

Browse teams by sport and ask to join, or create your own and you're its captain. Bring players in, give them a role, and set the lineup before every match.

  • Roles that match real teams

    Captain, co-captain, manager and member, each with the right permissions. Captaincy can be handed over at any time.

  • Join requests

    Players ask to join. Captains, co-captains and managers approve or decline them.

  • Lineups on a formation

    Set your football lineup on a formation that fits the format, from 5v5 to 11v11.

  • Fans and history

    Supporters can fan your team and follow its match history. Members get a team chat, and fans get a room of their own.

04 Feed & stories

Every match leaves a highlight reel.

Finished matches land in the feed as result cards, next to posts, training and clips from the people and teams you follow.

  • Results in the feed

    Every completed match shows up as a card with both teams, the final score and who won.

  • Stories for 24 hours

    Share a moment from the sideline. It disappears after a day.

  • Posts, likes, comments and shares

    Post photos and updates, then react to your teammates' and rivals' posts. Match clips stay with the match they came from.
